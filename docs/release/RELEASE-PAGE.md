One Apple-style battery per AI account, on your Mac's menu bar. Click for the panel: every account's windows exactly as the provider reports them.

**Install.** Download `QotaFolio-{{VERSION}}.zip`, double-click it, and drag QotaFolio to your Applications folder. The first time you open it, macOS asks once — *"QotaFolio" is an app downloaded from the internet. Are you sure you want to open it?* — and notes that Apple checked it for malicious software. Click **Open**. Then add an account: name it, pick the provider by its mark, sign in in your browser, and you are back with numbers in under a minute. Requires macOS 26.

What QotaFolio does with your credentials, where a Claude account stands with Anthropic, and how releases are signed and updated are in the [README](https://github.com/c2coast/QotaFolio#readme). Worth reading before you sign an account in.
