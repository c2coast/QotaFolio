# QotaFolio {{VERSION}}

The first release. One Apple-style battery per AI account on your menu bar; click for the panel.

- Anthropic (Claude) and ChatGPT accounts, several of each, in the order you keep them.
- The panel shows each account's windows as the provider reports them — percentage used, a level bar, and the reset as a live countdown.
- Click a card for the instrument: the day so far as a line, and the projected end of the window with an honest range.
- A Control for the menu bar or Control Center, a desktop widget, Spotlight ("check my quota"), and `qota status` in Terminal.
- Alerts that inform and never instruct. A privacy blanket while your screen is shared.
- Notarized, sandboxed, updated through a signed Sparkle feed. Nothing leaves your Mac.

QotaFolio requires macOS 26. It is an independent product, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic or OpenAI.
